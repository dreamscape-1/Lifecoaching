Why I do this

I spent years leading teams. Somewhere along the way I noticed that the moments I remember have nothing to do with targets or milestones. They're the quiet conversations, when someone finally said out loud what they'd been carrying, and walked away a little lighter.

People have always found it easy to open up to me. I've never taken that lightly. It's a kind of trust, and it matters more to me than any title I've held.

So I'm doing more of it, on purpose. I coach people through burnout and life or career transitions because I believe no one should have to work through a hard chapter alone.

I believe one honest conversation can change someone's week, and sometimes their whole direction.

You're not broken. You're between chapters.

The exhaustion, the doubt, the restlessness: they aren't proof that something is wrong with you. Often they're a sign that the old chapter has ended and the next one hasn't started yet.

That in-between is uncomfortable. It's also where the most honest work happens, and where a lot of people finally find their footing.

Questions you might have

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching looks forward: getting clear on where you are, where you want to go, and what to do next. It isn't treatment for mental health conditions. If you're dealing with depression, trauma, or anxiety that's affecting daily life, a licensed professional is the right support, and I'm happy to help you work out what fits.

What if I don't know what I want?

That's exactly where most people begin. You don't need a goal to book a session. Feeling lost is a perfectly good reason to talk.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. It's 30 minutes, with no payment details and no obligation. It's simply a chance for us both to see whether working together makes sense.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Nothing at all. Come as you are. If it helps, you can jot down a few words about what's been on your mind, but it's completely optional.

What happens after the free session?

If we both feel it's a good fit, we can talk about what working together might look like. If not, there's no follow-up pressure. You're free to take your time or walk away.

Will what I share stay private?

Yes. What you share with me stays between us. It's a safe space to speak openly.

Whenever you're ready, I'm here.
